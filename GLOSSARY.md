# Chongkran

Chongkran helps home cooks discover recipes and organize meals.

## Language

**Home cook**:
A person finding and preparing meals at home; Chongkran's primary audience.

**Recipe discovery**:
Finding something to cook by browsing, searching, and filtering Chongkran's recipes.

**Unpublished recipe**:
A pending or rejected recipe, visible only to its owner and administrators.

**Recipe view**:
A signed-in cook's first recorded visit to an approved recipe. Repeat visits by
the same cook do not increase its view count.

**Meal plan**:
A schedule of recipes for particular days and meals. Scheduling a recipe twice
requires its ingredients twice when generating a shopping list.

**Compatible quantities**:
Amounts of the same ingredient expressed in matching units or convertible metric
units. Ambiguous amounts such as "to taste" remain separate.
