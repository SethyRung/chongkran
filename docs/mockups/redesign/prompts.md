The 11 PNG boards cover all 24 page routes and show selected mobile layouts and component states. Sample data and secondary labels are illustrative. Board 10 shows the shared navigation and footer.

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. Buck’s Sauce informed the condensed type, isolated food photography, and saturated color blocks. [Design reference](https://buckssauce.com/)

Use a completely original wordmark, food imagery, text, page grid, and header for Chongkran. No reference brand artwork, promotional bar, packaging, mascot, or imitation of its unusual navigation and carousel.

```text
Art direction: vivid Southeast Asian food photography meets a confident, contemporary market poster. Burnt-charcoal #191512, toasted-cream #F5E6CF, turmeric yellow #FFBC34, chili vermilion #BD3427, muted aubergine #63354B, and leaf green #355130. Headlines use a strong tightly spaced condensed sans in bold uppercase, with selectively outlined large text. All forms and navigation use a highly readable friendly sans, normal casing. Most geometry is square with 4–8px radius. Flat and clear, some fine paper texture only on large surfaces, excellent contrast. Brand mark “CHONGKRAN” is a chunky condensed cream wordmark beside three compact hand-drawn red steam strokes. Make the visible UI rich in real original food photography, not stock-vector food.

Header: compact inky straight edge band, wordmark at left, middle links “Recipes”, “Categories”, “Meal Plans”, “Shopping List”, simple magnifier and outline “Sign in” at right.
Hero: dramatic two-column composition with the dark page stage on the left and distinct red angled color field peeking behind cutout food at right. Show a wide conspicuous cream search field “Search recipes or ingredients” and a solid turmeric “Find recipes” action. The right features a close, flash-lit photograph of a generous ceramic plate of “Nom banh chok”, with rice noodles, herb-heavy green fish curry, fresh herbs and cucumber. Food glistens naturally and overlaps the angled color field slightly. Keep the two columns visually balanced. Small cream underlined recipe link at lower right “Nom banh chok ↗”. The hero ends visibly within the upper half of the screenshot to encourage browsing and show the collection.
Then a toasted-cream band “Browse categories” with an “All categories →” link and four clean, equal panels “Weeknight”, “Soups”, “Grilling”, “Desserts”, each combining highly readable big type with a photography cutout and a distinct chili red / plum / green / turmeric color background. Below that “Popular recipes” with three original photo cards (“Fish amok”, “Beef lok lak”, “Chicken curry”), each with a real cream card base, short cook time and one heart save control. A compact charcoal footer with wordmark and functional links.
Designed like a real site, no presentation frame. Keep exact readable names, big readable search, strong editorial imagery, no added badges, editorial numbering, sales content, illegible type, or slogans.
```
